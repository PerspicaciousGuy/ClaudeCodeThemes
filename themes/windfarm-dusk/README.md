# Windfarm Dusk

An evening over a windfarm. The sky runs from lilac through pink to peach around a low sun, three ridges of plum hills carry rows of wind turbines that turn at their own speeds, two huge ones frame the edges, and clouds and a few birds drift across.

![Windfarm Dusk backdrop](../../docs/previews/windfarm-dusk.png)

It is a light theme and keeps the app light. The panels are frosted over the scene so text stays readable. Animations stop if your system asks for reduced motion.

Install: `/plugin install windfarm-dusk --marketplace PerspicaciousGuy/ClaudeCodeThemes` (see the [repository README](../../README.md#install) for the desktop app and shell steps).
